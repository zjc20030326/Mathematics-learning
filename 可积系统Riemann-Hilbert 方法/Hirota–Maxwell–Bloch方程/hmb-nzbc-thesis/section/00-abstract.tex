\begin{abstract}
Hirota--Maxwell--Bloch（HMB）系统刻画了计及三阶色散与自陡峭等高阶效应的光脉冲与两能级共振介质之间的非线性相互作用，在飞秒激光与掺铒非线性光纤传播中具有重要物理背景。针对两端趋于同相平面波的非零光场边界条件，本文利用规范变换、双叶谱曲面单值化与矩阵 Riemann--Hilbert 问题（RHP），系统建立了 HMB 系统的直接散射理论、逆向重构方法与精确多孤子构造方法。

首先，确定与非零边界条件相容的平面波背景与 Bloch 守恒约束，验证三阶演化 Lax 对的零曲率等价性，并借助常数相位规范变换将空间无穷远背景化为与时空无关的常矩阵，将双叶谱曲面单值化为复平面上的径向解析区域与换叶对称结构。其次，由端点归一化构建空间 Jost 矩阵，证明其在各解析域内的解析性、唯一性及分支点处的加权延拓性质，给出散射矩阵的对称约化、四点轨道离散谱及两处谱无穷远的渐近展开。进一步结合演化 Lax 方程与介质方程，推导散射数据与范数常数的时间演化规律，并证明共振极点与光谱分支交汇处的相容条件与零反射性质，从而消去演化方程中的奇异极点。在此基础上，建立单值化谱平面上的矩阵 RHP，得到光场由无穷远展开系数恢复、介质矩阵由共振原像取值恢复的重构公式，并导出透射系数的迹公式与两端同相相位条件。

在无反射情形下，对任意 $N$ 个完整简单四点轨道构造有限维留数线性系统，通过 Cauchy 矩阵的分块合同变换严格证明系数矩阵的行列式在全时空上恒正，并由此完整证明空间端态收敛性、Lax 对及零曲率条件的逆向恢复、原 HMB 方程与 Bloch 约束的严格满足以及直接散射数据的精确复原。作为一般理论的应用，给出单孤子解的紧致正交投影表示与一般复谱双孤子解的行列式构造，并通过三场分布图像展示了单孤子呼吸调制与双孤子弹性碰撞特征。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
The Hirota--Maxwell--Bloch (HMB) system models the resonant interaction between an ultrashort optical pulse with higher-order dispersion and self-steepening effects and a two-level medium. Under nonzero boundary conditions where the optical field approaches in-phase plane waves at both spatial infinities, this thesis develops a rigorous inverse scattering transform and soliton reconstruction theory via gauge reduction, Riemann surface uniformization, and a matrix Riemann--Hilbert problem (RHP).

Starting from the compatible plane-wave background and Bloch constraint, we verify the zero-curvature formulation and apply a gauge transformation to reduce the spatial asymptotics to a constant background matrix. A uniformization variable maps the two-sheeted spectral curve onto four analytic domains separated by the real axis and a circle, inducing conjugation and sheet-exchange symmetries. We construct spatial Jost solutions via Volterra integral equations, establish their analyticity, uniqueness, and weighted extension to branch points, and derive scattering matrix reductions, quartet discrete spectra, and asymptotics at both spectral infinities. Combining the temporal Lax equation with the medium equations yields the evolution of scattering data and norming constants, while compatibility at the resonance preimages enforces diagonal scattering and vanishing reflection coefficients. Formulating the matrix RHP gives reconstruction formulas for the optical field at infinity and the medium matrix at the resonance preimages, alongside the trace formula and in-phase condition.

In the reflectionless $N$-orbit case, the RHP reduces to a finite-dimensional residue system. Using a Hermitian block congruence of the Cauchy matrix, we prove strictly positive system determinants everywhere in space-time. Consequently, the reconstructed fields are globally smooth, satisfy the HMB equations, in-phase boundary conditions, weighted Sobolev regularity, and Bloch constraint, and recover the prescribed scattering data. Finally, we obtain a rank-one orthogonal projector formula for the single soliton and a determinant formula for the general two-soliton solution, illustrating their breathing and collision dynamics.
\end{abstract*}
