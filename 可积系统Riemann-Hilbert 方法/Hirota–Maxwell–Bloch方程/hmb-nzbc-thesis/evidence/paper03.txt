Inverse scattering transform and soliton solutions for the coupled
Hirota-Maxwell-Bloch system with nonzero background

Shuai Liu1 · Yaqing Liu1


Received: 8 April 2026 / Revised: 21 May 2026 / Accepted: 1 June 2026 / Published online: 14 June 2026
© The Author(s), under exclusive licence to Springer Nature B.V. 2026


Abstract
In this paper, we study the N-soliton solutions for Hirota-Maxwell-Bloch system (H-MB system) via Riemann-Hilbert
approach. By a Lax pair of this system, the inverse scattering method is employed to construct a Riemann-Hilbert problem
(RHP) for the H-MB system under a non-zero background, which is imposed only on the η component (with u, p →0 as
x →±∞). From this RHP, the long-time asymptotics of the soliton is derived, and the N-soliton solutions are obtained,
with explicit expressions given for the cases N = 1, 2, 3. Furthermore, the dynamical properties of the case where N = 1, 2
are investigated, and the corresponding three-dimensional plots are presented. Compared with the conclusions reported under
speciﬁc parameter choices, the results obtained in this paper are valid for general parameters α and β. This is of great
signiﬁcance for the study of nonlinear pulse propagation in optical ﬁbers.

Keywords Hirota-Maxwell-Bloch system · Solitons · Lax pair · Inverse scattering method · Riemann-Hilbert problem
